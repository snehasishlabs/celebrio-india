**[Action: Presenter navigates to the 'What-If Simulator' tab]**

>
> Anyone who has planned an Indian wedding knows that changes are inevitable. Imagine the family gets a sudden call:
> 1. Out-of-town relatives are arriving—the guest count increases from **150 to 180 guests (+20%)**.
> 2. At the very same time, a family financial constraint forces the budget down from **₹3,00,000 to ₹2,40,000 (a ₹60,000 cut)**.
>
> In an Excel sheet, this would take days of panicked recalculations. Let's do it in Celebrio India.
>
> **[Presenter adjusts Guest Count slider to 180 and Budget slider to ₹2,40,000]**
>
> Instantly, our deterministic **Scenario Engine** evaluates the impact:
> - **Zero Arithmetic Hallucination:** The total allocated budget is recalculated to **₹2,40,000** exactly.
> - **The 8% Contingency Shield adjusts dynamically to ₹19,200**.
> - **Confirmed Contracts are Protected:** The ₹60,000 venue deposit already signed with Sovabazar Heritage Estate is locked and completely untouched.
> - **Intelligent Scaling:** Flexible per-person categories like catering and sweets scale down from luxury plating to rich buffet formats to absorb the extra 30 guests within the smaller budget.
>
> Notice the **Visual Trade-Off Cards**: Celebrio tells the user *why* catering was adjusted and suggests specific alternatives, such as opting for seasonal Kolkata sweets rather than imported confectioneries.

> Most importantly: **The original plan is completely safe in preview mode**. Only when I click **'Apply Revised Plan'** does Celebrio commit these changes."
